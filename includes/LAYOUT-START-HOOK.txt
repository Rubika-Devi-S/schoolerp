Add this ONE line in includes/layout-start.php after bootstrap/database is loaded and before the page body is rendered:

require_once __DIR__ . '/general-settings-client.php';

This exposes globally:
- window.SCHOOL_GENERAL_SETTINGS
- window.SchoolSettings.formatDate(value)
- window.SchoolSettings.formatTime(value)
- data-school-default-time="start"
- data-school-default-time="end"
- data-school-time-value="HH:MM:SS"
- data-school-date-value="YYYY-MM-DD"

Time inputs filled by data-school-default-time remain editable by the user.
